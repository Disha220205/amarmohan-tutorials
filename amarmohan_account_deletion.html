Account & Data Deletion Request

Users of AmarMohan Tutorials can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion
  1. Send an email to the support address below.
  2. Use the subject: Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the phone number or account identifier registered with the app.
  4. We may verify ownership of the account before processing the request.

Data that may be deleted

Depending on the account and records associated with it, deletion may include the user's name, phone number, email address, student/parent profile information, academic records, attendance records, reminders, fee-related records, and other personal information associated with the account.

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes. Any retained information will be kept only as required.
